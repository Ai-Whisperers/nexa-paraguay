## Hidden Savings in Paraguay

Beyond the obvious categories, Paraguay offers significant "hidden" savings:

### No Property Tax
Property taxes in Paraguay are negligible — typically $100-300/year on a $200K property. Compare to $4,000-8,000/year in most US states.

### No Capital Gains Tax
Sell investments, crypto, or property at a profit? 0% capital gains tax in Paraguay on foreign assets.

### Lower Utility Bills Year-Round
No heating bills needed (temperate winters) and moderate AC costs. Compare to US winter heating bills of $150-400/month.

### Cheaper Services
House cleaning: $10-15/visit. Personal training: $15-25/session. Massage: $20-30/hour. All 60-80% cheaper than the US.

## Paraguay Residency for Crypto Investors: The Essential Guide

Paraguay has become the world's most attractive residency destination for crypto investors. Zero tax on foreign crypto gains, no CRS reporting, low cost of living, and citizenship in just 3 years.

But how do you actually get residency? What does it cost? What about DNIT Resolution 47? This guide covers everything.

## Why Crypto Investors Choose Paraguay

Before diving into the process, here's why Paraguay's residency is particularly powerful for crypto holders:

- **0% tax on foreign-source crypto gains** — territorial tax system
- **0% wealth tax** — no tax on your total crypto holdings
- **0% inheritance tax** — pass crypto to heirs tax-free
- **No CRS participation** — your home country is not automatically notified
- **Crypto classified as goods** — not financial assets, simplifying tax treatment
- **DNIT Res 47/2026** — reporting obligation only, NOT a tax
- **Citizenship in 3 years** — fastest path in the Americas

## The Two Residency Routes

### Route 1: Traditional Temporary Residency

The most common and affordable path. No minimum investment required.

**Requirements:**
- Valid passport (must have at least 6 months validity)
- Clean criminal record (certified and apostilled)
- Birth certificate (certified and apostilled)
- Marriage certificate (if applicable, certified and apostilled)
- Proof of sufficient income or assets (can be crypto holdings)
- Police clearance from country of residence
- Medical certificate
- Application fee payment

**Cost breakdown:**
- Document preparation and apostille: $500-$1,000 (varies by home country)
- Legal fees (Paraguay): $1,500-$2,500
- Government fees: $500-$700
- Translation costs: $200-$400
- Travel costs (2 trips): $1,000-$2,000
- **Total: approximately $2,900-$5,500**

**Timeline:** 3-6 months from start to cedula in hand

### Route 2: Investor Pass (Direct Permanent Residency)

Launched in 2025, the Investor Pass grants direct permanent residency.

**Investment Requirement:**
- $150,000 minimum in real estate, tourism, or business
- OR $70,000 over 10 years via SUACE (business investment visa)

**Cost breakdown:**
- Investment: $150,000+
- Legal fees: $3,000-$5,000
- Government fees: $1,000-$2,000
- **Total: $154,000+**

**Timeline:** 2-4 months

### Which Route for Crypto Investors?

For most crypto investors, the traditional temporary residency route is the best option. It costs $2,900+, takes about the same time, and leads to citizenship after 3 years of temporary residency.

The Investor Pass makes sense if you want permanent residency immediately and have the capital to deploy in Paraguayan real estate or business.

## The Step-by-Step Process

### Step 1: Document Preparation (Month 1)

The most time-consuming part. Start early.

**Documents needed:**
1. Passport (certified copy, notarized)
2. Birth certificate — obtain a certified copy, get it apostilled
3. Police background check — from your country of residence, apostilled
4. Marriage certificate (if applicable) — apostilled
5. Medical certificate — from a licensed physician
6. Bank reference letter — proving financial solvency (can reference crypto assets)
7. Letter of intent — explaining why you want residency
8. Passport photos (4x4 cm, white background)

**Important for crypto investors:** Your source of funds documentation should include exchange statements, wallet screenshots, and tax returns showing the legitimate origin of your crypto wealth. Paraguay is more flexible than most countries, but professional documentation helps.

### Step 2: Submission to Migraciones (Month 2-3)

Once documents are prepared, your Paraguayan lawyer submits them to the Direccion General de Migraciones.

**Requirements:**
- All documents must be translated to Spanish by a certified Paraguayan translator
- Original documents plus copies
- Application fees paid

**Processing time:** 30-60 days

### Step 3: First Trip — Biometrics and Interview (Month 3)

Once the application is preliminarily approved, you must visit Paraguay for:

1. Biometric data collection (fingerprints, photo)
2. Interview with immigration officials
3. Medical check (local doctor, ~$50)
4. Registration with DNIT for tax ID (RUC)

**Duration:** 3-5 days in Paraguay

**Minimum physical presence note:** There is no minimum stay requirement for maintaining residency. You must visit Paraguay once every 3 years to keep your residency active.

### Step 4: Approval and Cedula (Month 4-6)

After your first trip, the final approval process takes 30-60 days.

**Once approved, you receive:**
- **Cedula de Identidad** — your Paraguay national ID card
- **Residency certificate** — proves your legal residence
- **RUC (tax ID)** — needed for local transactions, crypto reporting

### Step 5: Citizenship (After 3 Years)

After 3 years of temporary residency, you can apply for naturalization:

**Requirements:**
- 3 years continuous temporary residency (no gaps)
- Basic Spanish proficiency (conversational)
- Clean criminal record in Paraguay
- Demonstrate ties to Paraguay

**Note:** The 3-year citizenship clock starts from the date your cedula is issued, not from application submission.

## DNIT Resolution 47/2026 Explained

This is the most misunderstood topic. Here's the truth:

**What it is:** A reporting resolution requiring crypto holders and platforms to submit annual declarations to DNIT (Paraguay's tax authority).

**What it requires:**
- Report crypto holdings exceeding $5,000 in annual transaction volume
- Provide wallet addresses and transaction summaries
- Identify counterparties where possible
- File annually

**What it is NOT:**
- NOT a crypto tax
- NOT a capital gains tax
- NOT a wealth tax
- NO tax liability arises from reporting

**Compare with DAC8:** DNIT Res 47 is Paraguay's equivalent of DAC8 — but with one critical difference. DAC8 enables tax collection (up to 48% in some EU countries). DNIT Res 47 enables only reporting. The underlying tax rate on foreign crypto gains remains 0%.

## Common Questions

### Do I need to live in Paraguay full-time?

No. There is no minimum physical presence requirement for maintaining residency. You must visit every 3 years to renew your cedula.

### Will Paraguay tax my existing crypto holdings?

No. Paraguay's tax system is territorial. Crypto acquired before residency remains foreign-source and is taxed at 0% when disposed of.

### Is DNIT Res 47/2026 a crypto tax?

No. It is an AML/KYC reporting requirement. You report holdings above $5,000 in annual transactions, but you pay 0% tax.

### Can I include my family?

Yes. Spouse and dependent children can be included in your residency application. Additional document and legal fees apply.

### How long until I can get citizenship?

3 years from the date your cedula is issued.

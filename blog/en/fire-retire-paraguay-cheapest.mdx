## FIRE in Paraguay: The Math That Changes Everything

The FIRE movement is built on a simple equation: save enough that your portfolio can sustain your lifestyle indefinitely. The 4% rule says you can withdraw 4% of your portfolio annually, adjusted for inflation, and never run out of money.

In many countries, a $500K portfolio generating $20K/year ($1,667/month) barely covers rent. In Paraguay, it funds a lifestyle that would cost $5,000-6,000/month in the US.

Here's why Paraguay is the best FIRE destination in 2026.

**The sweet spot:** $500,000. At $1,667/month, you live extremely well in Paraguay — modern 2-bedroom apartment, dining out regularly, gym membership, and international travel twice a year.

## Tax: The FIRE Killer

The single biggest expense for most FIRE retirees is taxes. In almost every country, your investment withdrawals are taxed as income. Paraguay's territorial tax system means:

- **0% tax on capital gains** from foreign investments
- **0% tax on dividends** from foreign shares
- **0% tax on interest** from foreign accounts
- **0% tax on crypto profits** (crypto is classified as goods, not securities)
- **No wealth tax**
- **No inheritance tax**

**Paraguay ties with Panama, UAE, and Costa Rica for tax — but beats them on cost and citizenship path.**

## Why Paraguay Over Each Competitor

### Paraguay vs Portugal

Portugal's NHR regime is dead. NHR 2.0 offers a flat 10% tax (not 0%), requires 183 days/year presence, and costs 2x more to live. Citizenship takes the same 5 years but requires actual residence.

### Paraguay vs Costa Rica

Costa Rica matches Paraguay on tax (0% foreign income) but costs nearly double to live and takes 7 years for citizenship with a 183-day presence requirement.

### Paraguay vs Thailand

Thailand has no path to citizenship. The Elite visa costs $18K-$143K with no permanence. Tax on remitted foreign income is 0-35% and recently tightened. You're always a guest, never a citizen.

### Paraguay vs Panama

Panama is 30-50% more expensive, takes 5-8 years for citizenship, and requires minimum $300K investment for the Friendly Nations visa.

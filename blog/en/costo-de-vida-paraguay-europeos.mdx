## Introduction

One of the main reasons Europeans relocate to Paraguay is the cost of living. But "cheaper than Europe" is not a number. This article provides specific, real-world monthly costs based on actual client data from 2025–2026. All figures are in USD unless otherwise stated.

## Housing: Rent by Neighborhood

Asunción's rental market varies dramatically by neighborhood. The most sought-after areas for expats are Villa Morra, Carmelitas, and San Roque — all in the central-southwest corridor of the city.

| Neighborhood | 2-bedroom apartment (USD/month) | 3-bedroom house (USD/month) |
|-------------|-------------------------------|----------------------------|
| Villa Morra | $600–$900 | $1,000–$1,800 |
| Carmelitas | $500–$800 | $900–$1,500 |
| San Roque | $400–$700 | $700–$1,200 |
| Las Lomas | $700–$1,200 | $1,200–$2,500 |
| Manors (villa-style) | N/A | $1,500–$3,000 |

To put this in perspective: a 2-bedroom in Villa Morra costs about what a studio costs in central Amsterdam or Berlin. A 3-bedroom house with a garden in Las Lomas costs less than a one-bedroom apartment in central London.

Most rental contracts require a deposit of 1–2 months rent and are denominated in USD, though payment is sometimes accepted in guaraníes at the exchange rate of the day.

## Utilities and Services

Electricity, water, gas, and internet for a 2-bedroom apartment in Asunción averages $120–$180 per month. Internet (fiber optic, 300 Mbps) is approximately $45–$60 per month from providers like Personal or Tigo. Electricity is notably cheap due to Paraguay's hydroelectric dominance (Itaipú dam) — you can run air conditioning 24/7 in summer for approximately $80–$120/month.

Mobile phone plans with 20–40 GB of data cost $15–$30 per month. A cleaning service (once per week, 4 hours) costs $60–$100 per month.

## Healthcare

Private health insurance for a healthy individual aged 35–45 costs $60–$120 per month from major providers like BNF or Grupo Médico. Family plans (couple + two children) range from $150–$300 per month. Coverage includes private hospital access in Asunción, outpatient care, and emergency evacuation within the country.

Out-of-pocket costs are also low: a specialist consultation costs $30–$60, dental cleaning is $25–$50, and a full blood panel is $40–$80.

International health insurance with worldwide coverage (excluding the US) runs $150–$300/month for an individual. Most European expats opt for a combination of local private insurance for routine care and international insurance for major events.

## International Education

This is the largest recurring cost for families. International schools in Asunción:

- **American School of Asunción:** $8,000–$12,000 per year
- **Colegio Internacional:** $6,000–$9,000 per year
- **Goethe Schule (German school):** $5,000–$8,000 per year
- **Lycée Français:** $6,000–$10,000 per year

Compared to European international school fees (often $20,000–$35,000 per year in major capitals), the cost is roughly half to a third.

## Groceries and Dining

A monthly grocery budget for a couple eating a mix of local and imported products is $300–$500. Local produce — meat, vegetables, fruits, dairy — is very affordable. Imported products (European cheese, wine, olive oil) carry a premium of 30–80% over European prices.

Restaurant prices: a meal for two at a mid-range Asunción restaurant costs $25–$50. A high-end dinner at a place like Tierra Corazón or La Cabrera is $60–$100 for two with wine. Lunch menus (menú ejecutivo) are $5–$10 per person.

## Total Monthly Budget

A realistic monthly budget for a European couple living comfortably in Asunción:

| Category | Budget (USD/month) |
|----------|-------------------|
| Rent (2-bed, Villa Morra) | $700 |
| Utilities + internet | $150 |
| Private health insurance (couple) | $200 |
| Groceries | $400 |
| Dining out (4x/month) | $150 |
| Cleaning service | $80 |
| Transport (rideshare, occasional taxi) | $100 |
| Miscellaneous | $200 |
| **Total** | **$1,980** |

Compare this to $4,500–$6,000 for a comparable lifestyle in Amsterdam, Berlin, or Paris. The savings are not marginal — they are structural.

## What Costs the Same

Not everything is cheaper. Electronics (laptops, phones), new cars, and flights to Europe cost roughly the same or slightly more. Amazon delivery requires freight forwarders. Import duties on personal shipments can be unpredictable.

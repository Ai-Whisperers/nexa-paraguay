## Detailed Breakdown

### 1. Paraguay

**The winner on speed, cost, and accessibility.**

Paraguay's territorial tax system means 0% on all foreign income. Local income (earned within Paraguay) is taxed at 10%, but almost all expat income qualifies as foreign-source.

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Foreign income tax | 0% |
| Path to citizenship | 3 years |
| Physical presence required | 0 days/year (1 visit/3 years) |
| Investment required | $0 (nominal application fees ~$2,900) |
| Cost of living (monthly) | $1,100-1,500 |
| Dual citizenship | Yes |
| Languages | Spanish, Guarani |

**Best for:** Anyone who wants a real second passport with minimal time, cost, and presence requirements.

### 2. Panama

**A strong competitor, but expensive and slower.**

Panama's territorial system is well-established. The Friendly Nations Visa requires $300K+ in investment or a 5-8 year wait for citizenship.

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Foreign income tax | 0% |
| Path to citizenship | 5-8 years |
| Physical presence required | 1 day/2 years |
| Investment required | $300K+ (Friendly Nations Visa) |
| Cost of living (monthly) | $1,800-2,500 |
| Dual citizenship | Yes |
| Languages | Spanish, English |

**Best for:** Those with capital to invest who prefer a more developed infrastructure.

### 3. UAE (United Arab Emirates)

**Zero tax, zero citizenship path, high cost.**

The UAE is the most famous zero-tax jurisdiction. Dubai and Abu Dhabi offer world-class infrastructure but no path to citizenship and a cost of living 2-3x Paraguay.

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Foreign income tax | 0% (no income tax at all) |
| Path to citizenship | No (extremely rare exceptions) |
| Physical presence required | 0 days/year (visa-based) |
| Investment required | None (employment or investor visa) |
| Cost of living (monthly) | $2,500-4,000 |
| Dual citizenship | Not for most expats |
| Languages | Arabic, English widely spoken |

**Best for:** High-income professionals who don't want citizenship and can afford Dubai lifestyle.

### 4. Costa Rica

**Territorial tax but high presence requirements.**

Costa Rica's territorial tax system is similar to Paraguay's, but requires 183 days/year physical presence and 7 years for citizenship.

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|

| Cost of living (monthly) | $2,000-3,000 |
| Dual citizenship | Yes |
| Languages | Spanish, some English |

**Best for:** Those who want a tropical climate and don't mind the higher cost and presence requirements.

### 5. Qatar

| Metric | Value |

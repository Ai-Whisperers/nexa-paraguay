## 4. SONIA'S PERSONAL STORY (for website content)

### 4.1 The Return (PG-friendly version)

Sonia lived 7 years in the Netherlands. She arrived without speaking Dutch, without contacts, without support. Initially worked in music, then retired to be a mother. She always knew she would return to Paraguay — her plan was to learn the culture and language deeply, pass it to her children, then return to be with her aging mother.

### 4.2 The Hard Truth (internal only — do not publish raw)

- She had a **nervous breakdown** in Holland
- "I wasn't happy there. I was going to end up badly if I stayed."
- Wanted to **reinvent herself** — same reason clients come to Paraguay
- Didn't want children growing up in Europe's coldness

### 4.3 Cultural Reintegration

- Hardest part: getting used to **dirty places** again
- But: stayed in bubble at first, so shock wasn't huge
- **Biggest shock for Europeans:** impunctuality and inefficiency (more than dirtiness)
- **Her advice:** "Learn to let go, don't be square. Enter the Paraguayan rhythm but without becoming Paraguayan. Become a relaxed European."

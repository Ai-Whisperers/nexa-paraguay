## 1. Residency Demand: Overall Market Size & Growth

### Record-Breaking 2025 Numbers

Paraguay's Direccion Nacional de Migraciones (DNM) reported historic figures for 2025:

| Metric | 2024 | 2025 | Change |
|--------|------|------|--------|
| Applications filed | 29,124 | **47,687** | **+63.7%** |
| Permits granted | ~28,464 | **40,600** | **+42.6%** |
| Backlog (gap) | 866 | ~7,087 | 8x increase |

Source: DNM Memoria 2025, published early 2026.

### Growth Trajectory (7-Year View)

| Year | Applications | YoY Change |
|------|-------------|------------|
| 2019 | 24,113 | - |
| 2020 | 12,247 | -49% (COVID) |
| 2021 | ~13,000 (est) | ~+6% |
| 2022 | ~22,000 (est) | +69% rebound |
| 2023 | ~26,000 | +16.5% |
| 2024 | 29,124 | +10.3% |
| 2025 | **47,687** | **+63.7%** |

The CAGR from 2019-2025 is approximately **12% per year**, but the pace accelerated sharply in 2025.

### Early 2026 Signal: Acceleration Continues

In the first 20 days of January 2026, DNM recorded **2,817 new residency applications** — a **79% increase** over the same 20-day period in 2025.

The Paraguay Sovereign "Momentum Index" (composite of application volume + system throughput) rose from **59.5 (2024)** to **79.8 (2025)**.

### Government Projection

The Paraguayan government projects that total residencies granted could reach **80,000 annually by 2026** (per National Migration Director Jorge Kronawetter).

## 4. Paraguay's Territorial Tax System: Appeal Analysis

### Core Mechanics

| Feature | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Foreign-sourced income | **0% tax** |
| Local personal income (IRP) | 8-10% progressive (above ~$12k/yr) |
| Corporate tax (IRE) | 10% flat |
| Simplified regime (IRE Simple) | 3% on gross revenue (for turnover <~$270k/yr) |
| VAT (IVA) | 10% |
| Capital gains (local) | 8% |
| Dividend withholding (residents) | 8% (was 15%, under Investor Pass) |
| Wealth tax | **None** |
| Inheritance tax | **None** |
| Gift tax | **None** |

Source: Law 6380/2019, Paraguay Sovereign, GoParaguay.

### Key Differentiators

1. **No minimum day requirement** for tax residency — unlike almost every other country. No 183-day rule. One visit every 3 years suffices.
2. **No sunset clause** — this is Paraguay's permanent tax code, not a temporary program (unlike Portugal NHR which ended).
3. **30+ year history** — the territorial system has been in place since 1991.
4. **Legitimate and recognized** — not a tax haven label; Paraguay is on OECD white lists, has DTAs with Spain, Chile, UAE, Uruguay, Qatar, Taiwan.
5. **No wealth or inheritance tax** — particularly appealing to European clients facing 20-40% inheritance/wealth taxes at home.

### Comparative Advantage

| Program | Foreign Income Tax | Min Investment | Physical Presence | Path to Citizenship |
|---------|-------------------|----------------|------------------|-------------------|
| Paraguay | 0% (territorial) | $0 (standard) / $70k-$200k (investor) | 1 visit / 3 years | 3 years PR |
| Panama | Territorial | $200k+ real estate | 183 days/yr | 5 years |
| Portugal NHR (ended) | 20% flat | N/A | 183 days/yr | 5 years |
| Spain Beckham Law | 24% flat | N/A | 183 days/yr | 10 years |
| UAE | 0% | N/A | 183 days/yr | No citizenship |
| Costa Rica | Territorial | $60k+ | 183 days/yr | 7 years |
| Georgia | 0% (non-res) | Variable | 183 days/yr | 10 years |

### Risk Factors

- **Nexus risk:** If services are delivered from Paraguayan territory, DNIT may classify foreign-client income as Paraguayan-source (Consulta Vinculante No. 403). Requires careful structuring.
- **Limited DTA network:** Only 6 double tax treaties. This can create challenges for exiting high-tax EU jurisdictions.
- **No bilateral social security** with EU countries — double social contribution risk.
- **Potential OECD pressure:** Pillar Two global minimum tax could create pressure on the 10% corporate rate, though individual territorial taxation is less threatened.

## 5. Recent Policy Changes Affecting Foreign Residency

### Law 6984/22 (October 2022) — Foundational Reform

| Aspect | Before | After |
|--------|--------|-------|
| Entry point | Direct permanent residency possible | Temporary residency mandatory (2 years) |
| Processing time | 3-6 months to permanent | 2+ years to permanent eligibility |
| Bank deposit | $5,000 USD required | **Eliminated** |
| Medical exam | Required | **Eliminated** |
| ID card (Cedula) | Only after PR | Available with temporary residency |
| Work rights | Not guaranteed | "Residencia precario" guarantees work rights |
| Income proof | Required | **Not required** for standard route |

### Late 2024 Changes

- **No bank deposit required** for permanent residency (the $5k requirement was fully eliminated).
- **No medical certificate** required.
- **New digital ID card** (microchip format) introduced September 2024 — requires in-person biometrics, creating a 90-day gap between application and ID issuance.

### January 2026: Decree No. 5278 for Venezuelan Citizens

New restrictions on Venezuelan applicants — documents outside 9 months rejected. Not directly relevant to European clients.

### April 17, 2026: Paraguay Investor Pass (Major Launch)

The biggest recent policy development. A new **direct-to-permanent-residency** program for investors:

| Route | Minimum Investment | Job Requirement |
|-------|-------------------|----------------|
| Tourism projects | $150,000 | None |
| Real estate | $200,000 | None (30% paid upfront = $60k minimum) |
| Stock exchange (BVPASA) | $200,000 | None |
| SUACE (existing) | $70,000 | 5 formal jobs over 10 years |

**Key benefits:**
- Direct permanent residency (skip 2-year temporary phase)
- Reduced dividend tax: 15% → 8%
- Mostly digital via SUACE
- One visit every 3 years to maintain
- Combinable investment types
- Processed in ~5 business days (certificate) + 3-6 months (residency)

### Ongoing Digitalization

DNM is implementing a fully digital residency system. Pilot phase expected mid-2026.

### Projected Processing Times (Current)

- Standard temporary residency: 60-90 days
- SUACE investor route: 45-60 days
- New Investor Pass: ~5 days for cert + 3-6 months for PR
- Conversion temp→perm: After 21 months of temp residency

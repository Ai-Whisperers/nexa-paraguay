## 1. DUTCH ENTREPRENEUR / FREELANCER (ZZP'er)

### What's Driving Them

**The "Box 3 Perfect Storm"** is the #1 trigger. Multiple converging pressures:

- **Box 3 wealth tax overhaul**: The Dutch Supreme Court (2021) ruled deemed-return taxation unlawful. A new "Actual Return" system (Wet werkelijk rendement box 3) passed the Tweede Kamer on Feb 12, 2026, effective Jan 1, 2028. Under the new system: 36% tax on actual returns including UNREALISED capital gains on liquid assets. The tax-free allowance shifts from ~EUR 59,357 capital to only EUR 1,800 annual return. A couple with a EUR 1M portfolio could owe EUR 34,704/year on unrealised gains.

- **ZZP crackdown (Handhaving DBA)**: Jan 1, 2025, the Belastingdienst began enforcing stricter rules against false self-employment. Result: 62,000 ZZPers exited the market in 2025. 59,000 transitioned to employee status in Q1 2025 alone. New entries dropped 59% in Q4 2025 vs Q4 2024. Youth ZZPers (15-27) declined 18%.

- **Self-employed deduction halved**: From EUR 2,470 (2025) to EUR 1,200 (2026).

- **30% ruling changes**: Partial non-resident status for Box 3 abolished as of 2025 for most. Expats who started before 2024 have transition period through 2026 only.

- **Exit tax**: 5%+ shareholders face exit tax (26.9-33%) on unrealised gains when emigrating outside EU. Security required for non-EU destinations.

**Key Stat**: 1 in 5 Dutch ZZPers (20%) seriously considered moving their business abroad in the last 12 months (Bunq/Panelwizard survey, June 2025). Among young entrepreneurs (under 35), 45% actively consider international expansion or emigration.

### Income Range & Willingness to Pay

- **Typical income**: EUR 60,000 - EUR 200,000/year
- **Average tax savings from PY residency**: USD $40,000 - $160,000+ annually vs Dutch system (paraguaytax.com)
- **Willingness to pay for residency**: High - EUR 3,000-8,000 for full service is easily justified by first-year tax savings
- **Decision speed**: Fast (3-6 months from research to application) -- the "window is closing" urgency is real

### Decision Triggers

2. **Tax deadline pressure**: Dutch tax season (March-May) triggers purchase decisions
3. **Concrete fear**: Being taxed on unrealised gains they can't sell (startup equity, illiquid shares)
4. **The "Toine Manders effect"**: Dutch tax expert Toine Manders is a key influencer -- his name/content drives trust
5. **Peer network**: 26% of ZZPers know at least one fellow entrepreneur who already left

## 4. ENGLISH TAX OPTIMIZER / EARLY RETIREE

### UK Non-Dom Changes (2025-26)

**The April 2025 abolition of the non-dom regime is the biggest driver**:

- **What changed**: Remittance basis abolished. Replaced by residence-based system. From April 6, 2025, all UK residents taxed on worldwide income as it arises.

- **4-year FIG regime**: New arrivals can claim relief on foreign income/gains for first 4 years if non-resident for prior 10 years. But existing non-doms who've been in UK longer don't qualify.

- **IHT bombshell**: Inheritance tax shifted from domicile-based to residence-based. Long-term resident = 10 out of last 20 years in UK. IHT tail extends 3-10 years after leaving. Non-UK assets now in scope.

- **Temporary Repatriation Facility (TRF)**: 12% rate on pre-2025 FIG for 2025-26 and 2026-27; 15% for 2027-28.

- **Affected**: ~74,000 non-doms (HMRC 2022/23). ~9,300 ineligible for FIG regime. ~14,800 eligible for FIG.

**The Exodus Numbers**:
- 10,800 millionaires left UK in 2024 (157% increase YoY) -- Henley & Partners
- 16,500 projected to leave in 2025 (carrying $91.8B in wealth)
- 257,000 British nationals moved abroad in year to Dec 2024
- Nearly 1M British departures since 2021
- 4,400+ company directors left in past year (Companies House)
- April 2025 departures were 75% higher than April 2024

### UK FIRE Movement

- **Typical FIRE targets**: UK FIRE seekers need ~GBP 500K-1.5M depending on lifestyle
- **State Pension**: GBP 11,502/year (2025/26) from age 67 -- valuable safety net
- **For early retirees**: PY's 0% tax on foreign income means pension/investment income is tax-free in PY (still potentially taxable in UK if UK-sourced)
- **Current situation**: UK state pension (triple locked) + PY low cost of living = very comfortable lifestyle
- **Challenge**: UK pension income remains UK-taxable under UK-PY DTA. But other investment income can be structured tax-efficiently.

### How PY Helps with FBAR, FATCA, FEIE

**FEIE (Foreign Earned Income Exclusion)**:
- Excludes up to ~$132,900 (2026 projected) of foreign earned income from US federal tax
- Qualifying: Physical Presence Test (330 days abroad) or Bona Fide Residence Test (full tax year in PY)
- Key: PY residency helps meet Bona Fide Residence Test
- Caveat: Self-employment tax (~15.3%) still applies on net self-employment income
- Investment income (dividends, capital gains, crypto) CANNOT be excluded

**FBAR (FinCEN Form 114)**:
- Required if foreign accounts exceed $10,000 aggregate at any point
- PY bank accounts trigger this requirement
- Penalties for non-compliance: up to $10,000/year non-willful; $100K+ or 50% of account willful

**FATCA (Form 8938)**:
- Thresholds: $200K end of year / $300K any time (single, abroad)
- Reports specified foreign financial assets
- PY banks comply with FATCA

**How PY specifically helps**:
- PY's 0% tax on foreign income means no double taxation on excluded income
- PY territorial system + FEIE = $0 US tax on up to $132,900 of foreign earned income
- PY has NO tax treaty with US (generally advantageous -- no conflicts)
- PY allows US citizens to open bank accounts (FATCA applies but manageable)
- No wealth tax or inheritance tax in PY preserves capital

### Do US Expats Need Different Services?

**YES -- US expats have unique requirements**:

1. **FBAR/FATCA compliance**: Must be built into service packages
2. **FEIE qualification support**: Day-count tracking, bona fide residence documentation
3. **IRS filing**: MUST continue filing US returns -- can't just "disappear"
4. **State tax**: "Sticky states" (CA, NY) may continue taxing -- need domicile planning
5. **Self-employment tax**: FEIE doesn't eliminate SE tax -- need S-Corp/LLC structuring
6. **Exit tax**: For high-net-worth individuals (net worth >$2M or 5-year avg tax >$200K) -- Form 8854
7. **Social Security**: Can receive in PY via direct deposit to US bank account
8. **Medicare**: Does NOT cover outside US -- need international health insurance
9. **FBI background check**: Unique requirement for PY residency (federal-level authentication)
10. **No tax treaty**: Means more complexity for investment/crypto income

1. **Toine Manders / Nozick Consulting** - Already engaged with Project Paraguay; natural partner for Nexa. He does webinars, accompanies Expeditions, and reaches wealthy Dutch considering tax emigration.

2. **Ab Flipse / GRYP** - Already visited Paraguay, discussed it extensively on his platforms (Brands & Flipse podcast, Ongehoord Nederland). Reaches financially-savvy, exit-minded Dutch. Natural cross-referral partner.

3. **Dutchies.app (Paraguay WhatsApp group)** - Massive reach (500K+ users globally), very easy to get listed. Should be a top priority for Nexa presence.

4. **MigratieMeesters** (Julien & Melvin) - Direct competitor in Dutch market. Worth monitoring. They have 400+ Telegram members, active WhatsApp group, active blog.

5. **EigenWijsReis** (Belgian family blog) - Unique Belgian perspective, growing audience, potential guest content partnership.

6. **Move Out Of Europe** (Elke & Chris) - Targeted at $1M+ HNW individuals. Premium partnership opportunity for high-value client referrals.

7. **Potkaars Podcast** - Already covered Paraguay in 3 episodes. Good for reaching Dutch considering emigration.

8. **Project Paraguay** - The dominant player. Competitor but also potential referral source for complementary services (Nexa could offer what they don't).

### Media Channels Summary:
- VPRO documentary "Wakker in Paraguay" (5 episodes, NPO 2) - MASSIVE reach, national TV
- AD/BN DeStem/Gelderlander - Major newspaper coverage
- Multiple podcasts (Potkaars, Praatkast, Zo Word Je Steenrijk, Brands & Flipse)

- "Wakkere Nederlanders" - Politically motivated, freedom-seeking (largest segment)

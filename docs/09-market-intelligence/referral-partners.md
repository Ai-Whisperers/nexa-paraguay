Each partner agreement should include:

- **Referral fee**: % of client fee (typically 15–20%)
- **Attribution window**: 12 months (credit for leads who convert within 1 year)

- **Exclusivity**: Non-exclusive (encourage partners to work with multiple providers)
- **Co-marketing**: Joint webinars, content, or events
- **Reporting**: Monthly lead and conversion reporting

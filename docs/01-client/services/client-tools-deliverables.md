## 6. TAX RESIDENCY EXPLAINED (simple version for clients)

**In 30 seconds:**
- Paraguay only taxes money you earn **inside Paraguay**
- Money you earn from **outside Paraguay** (Dutch clients, freelance, investments) = **0% tax**
- You pay 10% on any income from Paraguayan sources
- No wealth tax, no inheritance tax, no capital gains tax

**What this means for you:**
- A Dutch freelancer earning €120K/year working from Asunción → **€0 PY tax** (was ~€59K in NL)
- Your savings and investments grow **tax-free**
- When you buy property, you pay 1.5% transfer tax (one-time) + 0.5-1% annual property tax
- When you sell property, 10% on the gain only

**What you still have to deal with:**
- 10% IVA (VAT) on most purchases
- Annual property tax (~0.5-1% of fiscal value)
- Proof of residency to maintain your status

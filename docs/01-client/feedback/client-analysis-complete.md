## Executive Summary

Nexa Paraguay is a relocation services startup founded by Sonia — a Paraguayan who lived in the Netherlands, went through the emigration process, and now helps Europeans do the same in reverse. The business is in pre-launch phase: no paying clients through the website yet, but Sonia has personally helped **10 Dutch people** relocate to Paraguay already.

**Core tension:** She wants a professional, trustworthy website that reflects a real business. But without real clients, data, or case studies, every claim risks being false. Sonia has a strong ethical compass — she'd rather have an empty section than a fake one.

## What Sonia Wants (from her own words)

*"Yo por haber vivido en el extranjero, en Holanda, y haber pasado por el proceso de emigrar, sé lo que se siente llegar a un país y no tener nadie que te guíe, sobre todo si no hablas el idioma."*

*"La idea es acompañarle a lo largo de su proceso de emigración al Paraguay con la parte de documentación, con buscar un lugar donde afincarse, con sus registros... Es un proceso que dura hasta un año, entonces es acompañarle de cerca, no es solamente sacarle documentos y luego me olvido."*

**Her vision:**
- Not a transactional paperwork service — a **long-term guide** (up to 1 year)
- Someone who **speaks the language** (Dutch, Spanish, English) and **knows the experience**
- A companion through the full journey: docs → banking → housing → settling in

**What she rejected (via Luana):**
- Fabricated stats ("47.000 residencias", "63% crecimiento", "+189% comunidad")
- Testimonials with no real clients
- Gimmicks: intake wizard, tax calculator, price displays
- Overcomplicated nav: Programas, Benelux, Recursos mega-menu

## BUSINESS MODEL — COMPLETE (from May 10 transcription)

### Core Service: Residencia Estándar — **$1,500**

This is Sonia's flagship offering. **She explicitly asked this number NOT appear on the public website.** It is internal knowledge for the business plan.

**What $1,500 includes:**
- Official translation of documents by a registered public translator in Paraguay
- Physical accompaniment to every government office (Interpol, Migraciones)
- Obtaining the birth certificate locally
- Obtaining the Interpol certificate locally
- Immigration entry processing
- Residence application submission
- Airport pickup on Day 1
- Local phone chip (SIM card) for immediate connectivity
- All documentation coordination end-to-end

**Timeline:** 3 to 6 months (vs competitor at $2,800 / 1 month 20 days)

### Competitor Comparison

| | Sonia (Nexa) | Competitor Agency |
|---|---|---|
| **Price** | $1,500 | $2,800 |
| **Timeline** | 3-6 months | 1 month 20 days |
| **Style** | Artisanal, personalized | Corporate, fast |
| **Difference** | $1,300 savings | Speed |

**Sonia's positioning:** Client who prefers to save $1,300 in exchange for a human, personalized process with direct accompaniment.

### Day 1 Protocol (Operational Detail)
1. Sonia meets client at the airport
2. If documents were sent ahead for translation → goes directly to Interpol and Migraciones
3. Goal: complete critical bureaucracy in **first 24 hours**
4. Gets them a **local SIM chip** immediately so they stop using European roaming

### Post-Residence Services (Second Service Tier)

After residency is submitted and documents are processing:

| Service | Pricing Model | Description |
|---------|--------------|-------------|
| **Bank account opening** | Per day of work (Sonia + driver) | Accompaniment to bank, translation, bureaucracy handling |
| **Vehicle purchase** | Free to client (Sonia takes commission from seller) | Takes client to most economical/reliable places |
| **Appliance purchase** | Free to client (commission from seller) | Fridge, freezer, electronics at trusted stores |
| **Property tours & verification** | Commission (2.75% of sale value) | Shows red zones/flood areas, prevents scams |
| **Spanish teacher search** | Free referral | Connects with trusted teachers |
| **Tours of supermarkets, cheapest places** | Built into accompaniment | Shows where to buy, what things cost |
| **Medical accompaniment** | Undefined — "did it from the heart" | Sonia took a client's friend to hospital by hand |
| **Social integration** | Free (calls) / Paid (mobilization) | Help joining clubs, signing up for activities |
| **School research** | Paid (one-time research fee) | Deep research of schools by city and budget |

**Sonia's pricing philosophy:** "I don't want to be abusive. The best propaganda is word-of-mouth." She wants first-world prices to feel reasonable while keeping clients so happy they recommend her.

### Commission & Referral Network Model

| Partner Type | Commission | How It Works |
|-------------|-----------|-------------|
| **Law firms / Accounting studios** | Commission (not charged to client) | Sonia presents 2 trusted firms; commission comes from the professional's margin |
| **Real estate intermediaries** | 2.75% (half of the intermediary's 5.5%) | Sonia contacts the intermediary, gets half their commission |

## Competitive Position (Sonia's differentiators)

1. **Lived experience** — She's not a lawyer who studied immigration law. She emigrated to the Netherlands, learned Dutch, navigated bureaucracy herself. That empathy is the product.
2. **Dutch-language service** — Rare in the Paraguay relocation space. Most competitors target Spanish/English speakers only.
3. **Long-term accompaniment** — Up to 1 year, not "get your papers and goodbye." This is the core of her pitch: acompañamiento de cerca.
4. **Single-trip model** — The first 24 hours consolidate all critical appointments.
5. **Full service range** — 9 services across 3 domains means no multi-vendor coordination for clients.
6. **Price advantage** — $1,500 vs $2,800 competitor (1.3K savings), 3-6 month timeline (more personal).
7. **Scam prevention** — Sonia's biggest pride: preventing clients from buying flooded or problematic properties.
8. **Family expertise** — School research per city+budget, medical accompaniment, social integration.
9. **Commission-funded extras** — Property tours, vehicle purchase, appliance buying cost client $0 because Sonia earns from seller commissions.

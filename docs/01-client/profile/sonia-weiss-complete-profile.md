## Her Story (from her own words, May 9 2026)

Sonia lived **7 years in the Netherlands**. She worked in the music industry but soon retired to dedicate herself to what she calls "la labor más bella" — being a mother. She always planned to return to Paraguay.

Her goal was to stay in the Netherlands long enough to learn the culture and language to pass it to her children, then return to be with her aging mother.

**The hardest part of returning to Paraguay:** "Reinventarme de vuelta culturalmente" (cultural reinvention). The paperwork was complicated, but being Paraguayan with family there made it easier.

**Her emigration experience is the foundation of Nexa Paraguay.** She arrived in the Netherlands without speaking Dutch, without contacts, without a support network. She went through it all herself, so she understands exactly what her clients are experiencing.

**Biggest professional pride:** "Haberles conseguido propiedades a buen precio evitando que les jodan" (getting them properties at good prices so they don't get ripped off).

**Her differentiator in her own words:** "Acompañamiento casi familiar" — almost family-like accompaniment.
